Digital technologies enhance economic resilience in Sub-Saharan Africa by fostering inclusion and long-term investment. Using a panel vector autoregression model for 29 countries (2000–2021), this paper shows that mobile telephony drives growth while Internet access supports sustainable debt-financed infrastructure.
