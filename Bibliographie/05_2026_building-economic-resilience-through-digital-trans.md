# Building economic resilience through digital transformation in Sub-Saharan Africa

**Année :** 2026
**Auteurs :** Awa Traoré, Thierno Thioune, Simplice Asungu
**Revue / Support :** Revue d'économie du développement
**Volume / Numéro / Pages :** Vol. 33, No. 2025/3-4, pp. 227-234
**Citations (Google Scholar) :** 0
**Lien / DOI :** https://shs.cairn.info/revue-d-economie-du-developpement-2025-3-page-227

## Résumé
Digital technologies enhance economic resilience in Sub-Saharan Africa by fostering inclusion and long-term investment. Using a panel vector autoregression model for 29 countries (2000–2021), this paper shows that mobile telephony drives growth while Internet access supports sustainable debt-financed infrastructure. JEL Codes: O33, G28.
